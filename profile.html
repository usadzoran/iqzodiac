PRIVATE BODY PHOTO SET · 6 VIEWS

How to take your photos

Follow the illustrated stance and check every image yourself. This app estimates image exposure and sharpness only; it does not recognize bodies or faces. A future model must pass a real face-anonymization check before it can be shown.

  1. Stand naturally and upright, with your arms slightly away from your sides.
  2. Frame your complete body, from head to feet, without objects blocking it.
  3. Use good, even lighting and a simple background; keep the camera at the same distance.
  4. Wear fitted clothing that does not hide your body shape; avoid loose clothing.
  5. Keep the same posture and lighting for every view.
  6. Do not use filters or edit the photos.
  7. Review each preview and retake any view before uploading. Photos stay private. No model is published unless a real service can verify face anonymization.
