PRIVATE BODY PHOTO SET · 8 VIEWS

How to take your photos

Follow these steps for a consistent set. Check every image yourself before upload; the app does not perform full-body or face recognition.

  1. Wear suitable, fitted sports clothing that shows body shape without being loose.
  2. Choose even, well-lit surroundings and a simple, uncluttered background.
  3. Stand upright with your arms slightly away from your body; keep the same pose, distance and light in all eight views.
  4. Keep your entire body from head to feet in frame; avoid anything covering it.
  5. Do not use filters or edits that change body shape.
  6. Keep your face and identifying facial details completely out of every photo. Review each preview before upload.
